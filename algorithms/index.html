Algorithms

This is the course website for Algorithms taught at NYU London. Roughly, these are topics I am planing on covering. Most of the class will be about algorithms - how to solve natural problems efficiently. We will also spend a small amount of time also studying complexity. How to show that a natural problem is hard to solve.

Algorithms (75% of the course)

  • Basics of BigO
  • Models Of Computation
  • Data Structures (Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs)
  • Dynamic Programming

Complexity Theory (25 % of the course)

  • Space and Time
  • Undecidability and reductions.
  • Discussion on P vs NP

Grading Policy

  • Scribe/In Class Participation: 10%
  • 6 Problem Sets: 60%
  • Final Project: 30%
