Munich Airport → Munich Hbf

From either Terminal 1 or 2, follow the signs down to the S-Bahn station (it's right at the airport). Take either the S1 or the S8 — both go to München Hbf, the airport is the first stop so you can't go the wrong way, and you just hop on the next departing train. The trip to the main station takes approximately 40 minutes, and between the two lines a train runs roughly every 10 minutes. At Hbf the S-Bahn arrives on the lower level, and your RJ 89 departs from platform 12 upstairs

Buy the tickets at the airport, tap works but carry some cash for emergency.
