Travel

I now live in Oxford where Vicky and I own a home. The village cat spends more time at our house than we do Below are few pictures of places we’ve been lucky to see. The low quality photos are taken by me. The pretty ones were captured by my wife, Vicky.

  1. My time as a high school soccer coach I coached high school for 3 years in Seattle for Soccer without borders. It was the best job I ever had.

  2. Road Trip Across Alaska An Irish man and a Brit rent an RV to drive across Alaska. What could go wrong?

  3. Backpacking In Glacier National Park Underprepared and underfed.

  4. Mailbox Peak Church: Modern medicines cure to all illnesses.

  5. Seattle To Portland 205 mile bike ride.

  6. Manchester Marathon My first marathon and Vicky’s fastest.

  7. Random Assortment Of Small Triathlon Races Many good days suffering on the wheel of my good friend Alexander Miles

  8. Colorado National Park, Pikes Peak, Mount Bierstead And some more peaks whose names I’ve forgotten. some more pics and some more. I’ve been to Colorado ALOT!

  9. Flat Iron Free Solo Otherwise known as the unbound stupidity of youth

  10. Summer In Berkeley At The Simon’s Institute Met a lot of clever people at the institute, and a few rattlesnakes in the Berkeley Hills. Long days reading followed by outdoor sunny swims. Californians are living the dream and they don’t even know it.

  11. Leavenworth, Washington First attempt at climbing outdoors with a rope.

  12. Edinburgh I feel about this city how many people feel about Paris. It is MAGIC

  13. Snowdonia National Park Under-appreciated part of the world.

  14. Lake District Steeper than it looks, and check out that dreamy cricket pitch.

  15. Devon/Cornwall/Exmoor Some photos of our wedding.

  16. Ragnar Pacific Northwest Trail Found a good group of people to sit in a van and run across the North West in turns.

  17. Spring time In Boston Grateful to Ran Canetti for hosting, and the bakers at Tatte.

  18. Madison, Wisconsin 7 well spent years at University

  19. Kurseong/Darjeeling, India: Years spent in my youth in the Himalayan foothills.
