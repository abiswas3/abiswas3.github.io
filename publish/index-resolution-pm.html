Resolution Lower Bounds

The paper in focus: Exponential Resolution Lower Bounds for Weak Pigeonhole Principle and Perfect Matching Formulas over Sparse Graphs

Notes For Rajko

  1. Resolution and PHP crash course: What is the resolution proof system, and the many variants of the Pigeon Hole Principle, and who done what. I write the definitions here in graph notation. This might seem unnecessary initially, but this is the most general way to express the axioms, and will help when we discuss sparse instances of PHP later (where each pigeon can only be matched to a restricted number of pigeon holes).

  2. Resolution Size Lower Bounds For The Most Basic PHP Principle: This is one of the first lower bound results by (Haken, 1984), which was subsequently simplified by (Beame and Pitassi, 1996). We present the simplified proof in these notes. The reason, I mention these notes, is that this lower bound is obtained by analysing the width of clauses in the refutation, where width is just how many literals are there in the clause. Later on more complex papers will use a different notion called pseudo-width. Good to understand how width is used, before understanding pseudo-width.

  3. Razbarov’s Pseudowidth + How to use it in expander graphs: Present a more general pigeon filtering statement by (De Rezende et al., 2020). Then we show use this general lemma to prove Razbarov’s result.c

  4. Short Proofs Are Narrow: The classic result by (Ben-Sasson and Wigderson, 1999) that underlies all of the above the proofs

  5. Closures Of Expander Graphs: A key feature in the proof is the idea of graph closure. Where does it come from, and what does it really get at?
